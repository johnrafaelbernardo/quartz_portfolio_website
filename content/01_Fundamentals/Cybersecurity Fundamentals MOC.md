# 🏛️ Cybersecurity Fundamentals

The foundation of IT security is built on understanding what we are protecting and why.

## Core Concepts

- [[The CIA Triad]]: The foundational model (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability).
- [[Risk Calculation]]: `Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact`.
- [[MITRE ATT&CK Framework]]: A knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques.

## Definitions

- **Assets**: Tangible (Servers) and Intangible (Data).
- **Vulnerabilities**: Flaws (Unpatched software, human error).
- **Threats**: Potential dangers (Hackers, natural disasters).
